Mileark Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Mileark records your drives so you can claim mileage on your taxes. Everything it records stays on your iPhone. We operate no servers, we have no account system, and your driving data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Data we collect

None. The developer receives no data from this app — no trips, no locations, no addresses, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising or tracking identifiers. There is no analytics SDK and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app.

Location data

Mileark uses location services to detect when you are driving and to measure the distance of each trip. Location is processed on your device and written to a private database inside the app's own storage container. It is never uploaded.

The app requests "Always" location access because a mileage log is only useful if it records drives you did not remember to start — that requires the system to wake the app in the background when you begin moving. You may instead grant "While Using the App" and start trips manually, or deny location access entirely and enter trips by hand; the app remains functional with reduced automation.

Mileark also uses Motion & Fitness (activity) data to distinguish driving from walking, so trips start and end accurately. This is processed on device and never stored beyond the current trip.

Addresses

To show readable start and end addresses, Mileark converts coordinates into street addresses using Apple's geocoding service, and lets you search for saved places using Apple Maps search. Those requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy; they are not associated with an account of ours, and we never see them. Saved results are stored only on your device.

On-device intelligence

Mileark can suggest a business purpose for a trip and can answer questions about your trip history in plain language. Both use Apple's on-device foundation models. Your questions and trip data are processed on the device and are not sent to any server, ours or Apple's.

Exports, backups, and sharing

When you export a mileage log or a backup file, that file is created on your device and goes only where you send it — Mail, Messages, Files, or another app you choose. Mileark does not upload exports anywhere. Once you share a file, it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or service you sent it to.

Because your log is stored on the device, it is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or local). Those backups are controlled by you and, if encrypted, by Apple's protections — we have no access to them.

Notifications

Mileark posts local notifications on your device when a trip is saved, so you can classify it. These are generated on the device. No push server is involved.

Children

Mileark is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your control

You can delete any trip or place inside the app, and deleting the app removes its entire database from your device. Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
