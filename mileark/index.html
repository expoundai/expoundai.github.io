Mileark

Every drive logged. Every deduction kept.

Coming to the App Store

Mileark records your drives in the background and sorts them into the categories a tax return actually asks for — so when filing season arrives, a year of mileage is already written. It runs entirely on your iPhone: no account, no cloud, nothing transmitted anywhere.

Starts by itself

Drives are detected and recorded without you touching the app — even when it's closed and your phone is in your pocket.

Knows your places

Save your office, clients, rentals, clinic. Trips there classify themselves — including the drive back and errands along the way.

Learns your habits

Correct a trip twice and Mileark handles that destination for you from then on.

Ask in plain words

"How many business trips this year?" Answered on device — every figure computed from your log, never invented.

Reports that file

IRS-ready PDF and CSV. Deductible trips itemized; personal driving reduced to the totals tax forms need.

Private by design

Your locations never leave the phone. There is no server to leak them.
